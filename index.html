Our story

From Limassol's Old Port to Paphos.

After years of grilling some of Cyprus's most loved kosher Argentinian food on the Limassol harbour, we've crossed the island. ASADO Express brings the same slow-cooked beef, hand-rolled kebabs, and fresh-pressed hummus — now in the heart of Paphos.

Every dish is a masterpiece; every sizzle is a harmony of cultural fusion. Argentinian fire meets Mediterranean soul, all kept strictly kosher.
