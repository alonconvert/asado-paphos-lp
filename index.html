I Our story

From the Old Port of Limassol, all the way to Paphos.

After years of grilling some of Cyprus's most loved kosher Argentinian food on the Limassol harbour, the family crossed the island. ASADO Express brings the same slow-cooked beef, the same hand-rolled kebabs, and the same fresh-pressed hummus — now in the heart of Paphos, a few minutes from the harbour.

Argentinian fire. Mediterranean soul. Strictly kosher.

Run by the same family. The same recipes. The same warm welcome — now under the Paphos sun.
